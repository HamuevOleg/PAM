RunBaza: race calendar and carpooling
A custom semester project topic based on the HamuevOleg/PAM README. The instructor confirms the topic at the Lab 1 defense.

Audience
Amateur runners in Moldova and nearby Romanian cities. The app brings race information together and helps runners find a shared ride to events outside Chisinau.

Screens
1. Home / Board: next race, number of races, available seats, longest distance and days until the start.
2. Race Index: races by season, with search and filters for month, distance, city, status and type.
3. Race Details: start time, meeting point, distances, entry fee, discounts, organizer notes and registration link.
4. Transfer: driver offers and passenger requests for a race, including time, pickup point and available seats.
5. My Plan: saved races and personal reminders.
6. Settings: theme, notifications and home city.
7. Login: sign-in or registration, added to meet the Lab 2 requirements.

Entities
Race: id, code, title, date, time, city, venue, distanceFromChisinau, distances, type, status, fee, currency, discount, organizerNote, registrationUrl.
TransferOffer: id, raceId, driverName, departureTime, pickupPoint, freeSeats, contribution. SeatRequest: id, raceId, passengerName, seats, pickupPoint.
User: id, name, email, homeCity. PlanEntry: id, userId, raceId, reminderDays. AppSettings: themeMode, notificationsEnabled, homeCity.

Plan for Labs 2-6
Lab 2: static Material 3 screens and mock data.
Lab 3: navigation and form validation.
Lab 4: state, filters, saved races and local storage.
Lab 5: remote data and authentication.
Lab 6: reminders, testing and final improvements.

Reason for choosing the topic
The project combines a sports calendar with the practical problem of getting to a race. A departures board layout makes race statuses easy to read and provides a basis for studying UI, state, storage and notifications.
