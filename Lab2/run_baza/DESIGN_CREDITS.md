## Flutter adaptation · 28 September 2026
Reference: https://github.com/dmitriaigenkiv/runbaza-app, main at c6ba2dd83bfd6ea072b6c1b8a685930290791b78.
The UI has been reimplemented using native Flutter Material 3 widgets. Reference files: packages/ui/src/event-design/{platform.css,industrial.css,Discovery.tsx,EventGlyph.tsx}.
Original line illustrations are adapted to CustomPainter, and cover/detail panels to a custom cut-corner clipper. No WebView, live service, authentication, real registration or booking is included. The six required project screens plus Login retain the original Lab 2 mock data.

## Moldovan distance illustrations · 28 September 2026
The grapevine, Chișinău arch, Old Orhei landscape and stylized Ștefan cel Mare monument are newly drawn native Flutter paths in race_motif.dart. No photograph, downloaded illustration or external image asset is embedded.

## Location-specific artwork · 28 September 2026
New original vector drawings: Iași Palace of Culture, Bucharest Romanian Athenaeum, Codrii forest, Dniester riverside and Valea Morilor park steps. The emblems are stylized interpretations and do not represent measured routes. No external image assets are embedded.
